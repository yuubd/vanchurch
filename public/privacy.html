Privacy Policy — Last updated July 22, 2026

PrayerRoom ("the App", "we", "us") is a prayer-sharing and community app for churches. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. We keep it simple: we collect only what the App needs to work, and we never sell your data.

Information we collect

  • Phone number — used solely to sign you in via a one-time SMS verification code (OTP).
  • Name — the display name you or your church administrator enters, shown to members of your community.
  • Community & cell membership — which church and cell group you belong to, and your role (member, cell leader, pastor, or admin).
  • Prayer requests — the prayer content you choose to write and share within your community.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or usage-tracking data.

How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • To display prayer requests, members, and cell groups within your church community.
  • To let members pray for and respond to one another's requests.

How your information is stored

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, our backend and database provider, and SMS verification codes are delivered by Twilio. Access to prayer requests is restricted to members of your own community through database access controls.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your prayer requests and profile are visible only to members of your church community, according to the sharing settings your church administrator enables. We share data only with the service providers listed above, strictly to operate the App.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can edit or delete your own prayer requests at any time in the App. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at the email below and we will remove your information within 30 days.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@vanchurch.app.
